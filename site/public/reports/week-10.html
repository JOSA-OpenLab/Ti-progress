Pick a Project, Write a Contribution Proposal

Done
Target: betaflight/betaflight · 11k+ stars, merges PRs almost every day, 349 open issues.
The project
Betaflight. Open source flight controller firmware for FPV drones, written in C. Basically the standard firmware people fly racing and freestyle quads on.
Why me, why this
I'm building my own quad right now (Nano flight controller, NRF24 remote), and I already had to debug an IMU sign issue on my own MPU6050. Betaflight is the same problem, just at real scale. Everything else this apprenticeship was Swift, Python, Rust. This is bare metal C and real-time control, the part of robotics I actually want to get good at.
The issue I'm targeting
#15526. A yaw sign got flipped when someone merged two trig calls into one (PR #14790). cos didn't care, it's an even function. sin did. Only shows up during GPS rescue when there's no compass on board.

Picked it because I already know this exact bug from my own IMU, and it's already fully diagnosed, so I can go straight to fixing it instead of hunting for the cause. There's a small second fix in the same area too, an old sin/cos pair left over in sdft.c.

Also keeping an eye on #15507, #15521, and #15527. Three separate reports of heading going wrong after an update. Might be one bug, might be three unrelated ones. Want to check once I actually understand the code.
12 weeks
  • 1-2: get it building locally, read the code, read a few old PRs to see how reviews work here
  • 3-4: fix and submit #15526
  • 5: fix the sdft.c leftover
  • 6-7: look into the heading reports
  • 8-9: learn the actual filter Betaflight flies on (Madgwick/Mahony), not just the fallback path I fixed first
  • 10: pick a real issue in that area, once I understand it properly
  • 11-12: ship it, write the whole thing up as a short talk
Risks
  • small maintainer team, could be slow to review
  • can't fly a real drone to test this, only math and unit tests
  • the heading reports might not be related at all
  • this codebase is way bigger than anything I've touched so far, first PR will take longer than it looks
Mentorship
  • someone who's actually flown FPV drones or built flight controllers, who can tell me if my fix makes sense in real flight, since I can't test GPS rescue myself
  • someone with a robotics/autopilot background (drones, rovers, whatever) who's worked with IMUs and sensor fusion, to check my math
  • someone plugged into the FPV/drone community who can intro me to a maintainer, or just point me to who's who in this space
I already have a few names in mind for some of this, happy to share if the coordinator can't find someone.

Dead Code Nobody Could Compile

Merged
Merged: betaflight#15820 · 11 deletions, 0 additions · approved by three maintainers and merged two days after opening.
Following through on my own offer
In September I corrected a maintainer's ticket (#15560) instead of fixing it, and offered to send the PR. A month later the ticket was about to close as stale. Then the maintainer re-checked it, his result matched my correction, and he left it open for anyone to take.

The firmware turns on each chip peripheral's clock through a table of tags, one set per chip family. One tag, RCC_AHB, and its five macros were left over from older chips that had a single AHB clock register. The ticket said they would fail to compile on three families. I had shown they could not compile on any family, because the register name they expand to does not exist on any supported chip.
Independent authors, independent reviewers
This time I used several AI models as independent authors and reviewers. Three models each wrote a fix without seeing the others, and all three arrived at the same 11-line deletion: the dead tag, its five macros, and four duplicated defines left behind by a 2023 copy-paste.

The one real risk was that removing a tag renumbers the tags after it on the F4 and H7 chips. Three more reviewers, each on a different model from the author it was checking, built up to 40 targets with and without the change and compared the firmware itself, not just whether it compiled.

What the RN: IGNORE label means
A maintainer labelled the PR RN: IGNORE within the hour. That only means it is left out of the release notes: deleting dead code changes nothing a pilot can see. Fixes that pilots do see, like last week's #15814, get RN: BUGFIX instead.

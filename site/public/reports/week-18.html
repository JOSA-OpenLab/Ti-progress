The Menu the CLI Left Behind

Approved
Open: betaflight#15932 · two lines · approved by haslinghuis and nerdCopter, waiting to merge.
The bug
The warning fires when the link reading is below the alarm. A CRSF link reports link quality up to 100 and RSSI up to 0 dBm, but the goggles menu let a pilot set 300 and +20, so the warning stays on even at a perfect link. The CLI dropped those limits in #9698 and #11062, and the menu never followed. The fix is two lines that give the menu the CLI limits.
The review changed the fix
haslinghuis asked for a link quality minimum of 0 to match the CLI. Before agreeing, I ran that change from every value the CLI allows, and it exposed a side effect: the menu steps by 5 and does not clamp, so an alarm of 3 set from the CLI wraps to 65534 on one press left, and the warning sticks on.

I wrote a clamp and pushed it. haslinghuis then asked why it covered only the 16-bit types and suggested doing it separately. That was fair: it had grown past what the PR was about. I took it out and saved it on a local branch for its own PR.
Running the real menu
In Week 17 I wrote that SITL compiles the OSD menus out. This week I turned them back on in throwaway builds, with OSD, menus and CRSF enabled and a few scratch patches that never touch the PR branch. A script played a radio sending CRSF over TCP and goggles over MSP displayport, then pressed through the real menu, saved, rebooted and read the value back.

Across 4 builds and 6 scenarios, 24 of 24 results matched my predictions, written before the runs. It is still a simulation. Nothing here has flown, and 2 of 26 launches came up with a blank screen for a reason I did not find.

The known limit
The PR that remains has a known limit, and I would rather log it than hide it. Until the clamp PR lands, an alarm of 1 to 4 set from the CLI still wraps on one press left, and 98 still steps to 103. MAX DISTANCE already wraps the same way on master today. nerdCopter's review found the same wrap independently and rated it minor, which also tells me the clamp PR should come soon. nerdCopter's review also noted the description had no test notes, so on 10 Nov I reran the SITL scenarios on the PR head and today's master, got the same results, and added a short test note to the description.
